James's 3-Day Workout Plan

Straight from my Shred app, with the weights I'm using right now.

Program: Build (muscle growth: controlled reps and lots of isolation)

Split: Push/Pull, 3 days a week, rotating Day 1, 2, 3

Time: about 75 to 90 minutes

How I lift: same weight each set while the reps drop (like 10, 8, 5). I go close to failure with good form and don't max out, to protect my shoulders, elbows, neck and back.

Superset means do those moves back to back, then rest.
